NordClipAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This policy explains how NordClipAI handles information when users interact with the software and supported third-party integrations.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features a user chooses, NordClipAI may process information needed to provide those features, including video-related data, account authorization information supplied by supported platforms, and technical information required to operate integrations.

2. TikTok data

If a user connects TikTok, NordClipAI will request only permissions needed for enabled TikTok features. TikTok information is accessed through authorized developer interfaces and used to provide functionality requested by the user.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate requested features, process and prepare video content, support authorized integrations, maintain reliability, and improve the service.

4. Sharing

NordClipAI does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a third-party platform when necessary to perform an action explicitly requested or authorized by the user, subject to that platform's policies.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and information handled by NordClipAI. Users should protect their accounts and never publicly disclose private credentials or access tokens.

6. Retention

Information should be retained only as needed for the applicable feature, operational requirements, legal obligations, or legitimate security purposes.

7. Third-party services

Third-party services such as TikTok operate under their own terms and privacy policies. NordClipAI does not control those services.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as NordClipAI evolves or its integrations change.

9. Terms

See the NordClipAI Terms of Service.
